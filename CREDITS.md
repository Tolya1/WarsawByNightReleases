# Warsaw by Night — credits and data sources

Warsaw by Night is a free scenery add-on for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. It is built from the open data
below. The data owners do not endorse this add-on.

## Street lamp positions

**Source: Urząd m.st. Warszawy** (City of Warsaw), city map service (mapa.um.warszawa.pl), layer of street-lamp poles
(`UZBROJENIE_SLUPY_LATARNIE`). Used under the service's re-use terms (mapa.um.warszawa.pl/warunki.html), which
require this source attribution. Lamp positions were derived from the published map layer; the add-on contains
derived lamp positions, not the original data.

## Map data

**© OpenStreetMap contributors.** Road network, street-lamp details (lamp type, double-arm poles), masts and towers,
lit sports grounds, fuel stations, airport boundaries and the city boundary. OpenStreetMap data is available under
the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

## Terrain

**Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii (GUGiK)**, numerical terrain model (NMT), used for lamp and glow heights. Open
data from the Polish national geoportal (geoportal.gov.pl).

## Light colours

LED colour temperatures (3000 K and 4000 K) follow **Zarząd Dróg Miejskich w Warszawie** (ZDM) LED luminaire tender
documents (2020).
